EVERYDAY FINDS GUIDE — WEBSITE STARTER
======================================

Yeh aap ki free Amazon Associates website ka starter template hai. Website English mein hai kyun ke Amazon.com audience aur Pinterest search ke liye English content aam tor par practical ho sakta hai. Aap content ko apni audience ke mutabiq edit kar sakte hain.

IS FOLDER MEIN KYA HAI?
- index.html: Homepage
- styles.css: Design aur mobile layout
- articles/: 4 starter buying-guide pages
- about.html: About page
- contact.html: Contact page (email placeholder badalna zaroori hai)
- privacy-policy.html: Starter privacy policy
- affiliate-disclosure.html: Affiliate disclosure page

ZAROORI: YEH ABHI LIVE WEBSITE NAHI HAI
Files download/unzip karne ke baad website aap ke paas hogi, lekin internet par live karne ke liye free hosting par upload karna hoga. Aap GitHub Pages ya Netlify jaisi static hosting service use kar sakte hain. Free plans aur unki conditions badal sakti hain.

PUBLISH KARNE SE PEHLE:
1. Site name, About page aur Contact page ko apne mutabiq edit karein.
2. Contact page mein apna working email add karein.
3. Articles mein apni original research, useful comparisons aur real product details add karein. Sirf template ko copy karke thin content ke taur par publish na karein.
4. Affiliate links tab add karein jab Amazon Associates aap ko tracking ID/link banane ki ijazat de. Placeholder ki jagah kabhi fake links na lagayein.
5. Amazon Associates ke current rules aur apne marketplace ki requirements check karein. Affiliate disclosure ko links/recommendations ke qareeb dikhayein.
6. Amazon product photos ko manually copy na karein jab tak aap ke paas unhein use karne ki permission ya Amazon ke approved tools na hon. Apni original images ya properly licensed images use karein.
7. Privacy policy ko apni actual site, analytics, cookies aur applicable legal requirements ke mutabiq update karein.

AMAZON ASSOCIATES KE LIYE:
- Website ka hona approval ki guarantee nahi.
- Original, helpful, public content aur Amazon ki current policies important hain.
- Amazon ke product prices, availability, ratings aur claims publish karne se pehle verify karein.
- Apne khud ke affiliate links se purchases kar ke qualifying sales banane ki koshish na karein.
- Affiliate links ko Pinterest par share karne se pehle Pinterest aur Amazon dono ki current policies check karein.

FREE HOSTING KA AAM TAREEQA:
- Kisi static hosting provider par account banayein.
- Is folder ki files upload/deploy karein.
- Provider ka diya hua public URL open karke test karein.
- Har page, mobile layout aur links check karein.

Yeh starter template hai, legal ya Amazon approval advice nahi.
